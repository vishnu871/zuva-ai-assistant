# Zuva Life Offerings Overview

## 1. Title
Offerings: Multiple Entry Points, One Connected Journey

## 2. Category
Programs / Services

## 3. Detailed explanation
People engage with Zuva Life in different ways, from short experiences and focused explorations to immersive cohort journeys. As priorities evolve, so do the possibilities. Someone may be considering a different way of working, exploring new avenues for contribution, or simply thinking more intentionally about the years ahead. Zuva Life offers several structured ways to explore these possibilities, all connected under the idea of designing the years ahead.

## 4. Key facts: the ways to engage
- **Spark Experiences** — Workshops, conversations, and small-group experiences that bring new ideas, perspectives, and possibilities into view. Often the entry point. See `spark-experiences.md`.
- **Purpose Pathways** — Focused direction tracks for those exploring advisory, entrepreneurial, creative, teaching, or portfolio roles, designed to help assess alignment and direction before a deeper commitment. See `purpose-pathways.md`.
- **Discovery Journey** — A structured 1:1 process (a 4 to 6 session journey) to help clarify what matters and identify clear next steps. The "Help Me Decide" starting point. See `discovery-journey.md`.
- **Zest Circle** — The signature cohort experience for people reflecting on what comes next in work, contribution, and life; combines reflective retreats and guided sessions. See `zest-circle.md`.
- **Community** — Ongoing connection, learning, and peer engagement (WhatsApp community and events). See `community.md`.
- **Partnering for Longer Lives (B2B)** — Zuva Life works with organisations rethinking how people live, work, and transition. See `events.md` and `contact.md` for engagement.

### Where do I begin?
- **Help Me Decide** — For those unsure what is next: start with the Discovery Journey, a self-reflection experience exploring interests, values, and sense of purpose before moving into action.
- **I Know What I Want** — For those already clear on a direction: explore Purpose Pathways and see how experience can be redeployed into meaningful next chapters.
- **Prefer to explore with others** — Join a Zest Circle cohort.

## 5. Services offered
Entry workshops, 1:1 clarity journeys, pathway exploration tracks, cohort programmes, retreats, community, and organisational partnerships.

## 6. Target audience
Midlife professionals at different stages of readiness, from "something has shifted" to "I know the direction I want to explore." See `audience.md`.

## 7. Benefits
A flexible on-ramp: people can start where they are and move between offerings as their thinking evolves.

## 8. Frequently asked questions
- **Where should I start if I have no clear plan?** Most people arrive with a sense that something has shifted rather than a plan. Start with the Discovery Journey.
- **Can I do just one workshop?** Yes. Spark Experiences are designed to stand on their own while opening up new perspectives.
- **Do the offerings connect?** Yes. They are multiple entry points into one connected journey.
- **Do you work with companies?** Yes, through the Partnering for Longer Lives track.

## 9. Important terminology
Spark Experiences, Purpose Pathways, Discovery Journey, Zest Circle, Community, economic longevity, midlife re-imaginers.

## 10. Internal links
`spark-experiences.md`, `purpose-pathways.md`, `discovery-journey.md`, `zest-circle.md`, `retreats.md`, `workshops.md`, `events.md`, `community.md`

# Zuva Life Offerings Overview

## 1. Title

Offerings: Multiple Ways to Engage With Zuva Life

## 2. Category

Programs / Services

## 3. Detailed explanation

People engage with Zuva Life in different ways, from standalone
experiences and focused explorations to deeper group journeys.

Someone may be considering a different way of working, exploring new
avenues for contribution, navigating a transition, or simply thinking
more intentionally about the years ahead.

Zuva Life provides several ways to explore these questions.

These offerings are multiple ways to engage with Zuva Life. They are not
required stages of a funnel and there is no mandatory sequence between
them.

## 4. Key ways to engage

### Spark Experiences

Workshops, conversations, learning sessions, and small-group experiences
that bring new ideas, perspectives, and possibilities into view.

Spark Experiences are designed to stand on their own.

See `website-content.md` and relevant Spark Experience knowledge.

### Purpose Pathways

Structured ways to explore directions for contribution and creating
value.

Examples include:

- Independent Director / Board Roles
- Coaching & Mentoring
- Entrepreneurship
- Creative Careers
- Teaching & Visiting Faculty
- Social Impact & Non-Profit Leadership

See `purpose-pathways.md`.

### Discovery Journey

A structured 1:1 process that helps people step back, take stock of
their experience and strengths, and identify possibilities worth
exploring further.

Discovery typically runs across 4–6 sessions.

See `discovery-journey.md`.

### Zest Circle

A small-group experience for people navigating transitions in work,
identity, and life direction.

It includes guided reflection, small-group conversation, facilitated
inquiry, peer learning, and other practices depending on the experience.

See `zest-circle.md`.

### Community

Ongoing opportunities for connection, learning, and peer engagement.

Specific current community activities should be confirmed from current
Zuva Life information.

### Partnering for Longer Lives

Zuva Life works with organisations thinking about longer lives, work,
leadership, contribution, learning, and transitions.

Corporate programs should not be presented as fully scaled or
standardised unless current information explicitly supports that claim.

## 5. Choosing an offering

There is no required sequence.

A person may choose an offering based on what they are navigating:

- Someone seeking individual space to reflect may explore Discovery
  Journey.
- Someone exploring a specific direction may explore Purpose Pathways.
- Someone wanting shared reflection may explore Zest Circle.
- Someone interested in a standalone topic, workshop, conversation, or
  learning experience may explore Spark Experiences.
- Someone looking for ongoing connection may explore the community.

These descriptions are ways to understand the offerings, not rules about
which offering someone must choose.

If the person's needs are unclear, Milo should ask one simple follow-up
question rather than forcing a recommendation.

## 6. Services offered

Zuva Life can provide:

- standalone experiences
- 1:1 clarity journeys
- pathway exploration
- small-group experiences
- retreats and journeys
- community experiences
- organisational partnerships

Specific current availability should be confirmed from current Zuva Life
information.

## 7. Target audience

People in midlife who are exploring questions around:

- work

- purpose
- contribution

- learning
- vitality
- economic confidence
- longer lives
- meaningful living
- community

## 8. Benefits

Zuva Life creates different ways for people to:

- reflect
- learn
- connect
- explore possibilities
- clarify what matters
- consider new ways to contribute
- shape the next chapter with greater intention

Specific outcomes depend on the offering.

## 9. Frequently asked questions

### Where should I start if I have no clear plan?

The Discovery Journey may be relevant because it provides structured
1:1 space to step back, take stock, and explore possibilities.

It is not mandatory. Other experiences may also be relevant depending on
what the person is looking for.

### Can I do just one workshop?

Yes. Spark Experiences are designed to stand on their own.

### Do the offerings connect?

The offerings sit within the broader Zuva Life approach, but they are
multiple ways to engage rather than required stages of one journey.

### Do you work with companies?

Yes. Zuva Life works with organisations around longer lives, work,
contribution, learning, and transitions.

Specific corporate offerings should not be described as fully
standardised unless current information supports that claim.

## 10. Important terminology

Spark Experiences, Purpose Pathways, Discovery Journey, Zest Circle,
Community, economic vitality, longer lives, contribution, midlife.

## 11. Important rule

Never describe the offerings as a mandatory funnel or sequence.

Never imply that someone must complete Discovery Journey before accessing
Purpose Pathways, Zest Circle, Spark Experiences, or Community.

Never force every visitor into Discovery Journey.
